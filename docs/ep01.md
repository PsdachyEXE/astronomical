### Sync points

- The cut to white lands on the onset of the word "white" in C03. Use the TTS provider's word timestamps; with none, fire at 85% of that clip's length.
- Narration starts 0.4 s after its cue begins, except C15, which waits 1.0 s. Pauses marked inside a cue are measured from the end of the previous clip.
- C12 has no narration. The bangs sit in silence and the drone, and nothing else is allowed to move.
- The three bangs are one sample played three times with 2% pitch variance, so they sound like the same fist.
